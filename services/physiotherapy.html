Our Services

01

Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy

Assessment and treatment of pain, stiffness, weakness, movement limitations, and musculoskeletal conditions.

02

Neurological Physiotherapy

Individualized rehabilitation for stroke, cerebral palsy, spinal cord injury, traumatic brain injury, and other neurological conditions.

03

Pediatric Physiotherapy

Developmental and functional physiotherapy for children with motor delays, cerebral palsy, developmental difficulties, weakness, balance problems, and mobility limitations.

Therapeutic Exercise & Functional Training

Balance & Gait Rehabilitation

06

Functional Rehabilitation

Task-oriented rehabilitation designed to help patients improve their ability to perform daily activities and participate more independently.

07

Home-Based / Online Guidance

Physiotherapy guidance and exercise programs for appropriate patients who require support outside the clinic.

Treatment Is More Than Exercise

The Goal Is To Improve Life & Independence

Effective physiotherapy begins with understanding the patient. We consider diagnosis, symptoms, physical impairments, functional limitations, daily activities, environment, and personal goals.

"The goal is not simply to reduce symptoms. The goal is to improve movement, function, confidence, independence, and participation in everyday life."

Your Journey to Better Movement

How It Works

Step 01

Assessment

We begin by understanding your medical history, physical condition, movement difficulties, functional limitations, and rehabilitation goals.

Step 02

Personalized Plan

A physiotherapy treatment plan is developed according to your assessment findings and individual goals.

Step 03

Treatment

Evidence-based physiotherapy interventions and therapeutic exercises are provided according to your needs.

Step 04

Functional Training

Where appropriate, treatment focuses on balance, gait, mobility, coordination, and activities of daily living.

Step 05

Home Program

You receive appropriate exercises and guidance to continue your rehabilitation safely outside the clinic.

Step 06

Monitor & Progress

Your progress is regularly reassessed and the treatment plan is modified according to your response.
